Idea 1: Visual Progress Indicators
Hypothesis: Adding a progress bar to the top of the board showing the percentage of "Complete" tasks will create a sense of accomplishment and encourage users to log in daily to "close" the bar.

Expected Impact: 10% increase in DAU and a higher rate of cards moving to the "Complete" lane.

Feature: A "Daily Shipping Goal" progress bar that resets every 24 hours, visualizing the team's collective throughput.

Idea 2: In-App Collaborative Comments
Hypothesis: If freight managers can tag each other on specific shipping cards, the platform becomes a communication hub, requiring users to log in to respond to mentions.

Expected Impact: 20% increase in session duration and a significant lift in DAU through notification-driven logins.

Feature: A "Comments" section inside the card modal with @mention capabilities and real-time push notifications.

Idea 3: Smart "Stagnant Task" Alerts
Hypothesis: Users often lose track of tasks stuck in the "In Progress" lane. Proactive alerts will bring users back to the app to resolve bottlenecks.

Expected Impact: 12% increase in status changes and improved task completion speed.

Feature: An automated system that highlights cards in Red if they haven't moved lanes in 48 hours, coupled with an morning summary email.
